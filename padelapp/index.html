Padel App – Score & Americano

Padel App is designed to make organizing and playing padel matches easier.

Features

  • Classic padel match scoreboard.
  • Americano match organization.
  • Player and court management.
  • Match history.
  • Timed Americano mode.
  • Wear OS integration.
  • Premium features available through a one-time purchase.
  • Optional 30-day Premium trial.

Premium

Padel App includes free features and optional Premium features. Premium can be unlocked permanently with a one-time purchase. No subscription or automatic renewal is required.
