About Beauty Shelf

Beauty Shelf helps you manage your personal product collection and routines without turning skincare into a strict checklist.

You can organize products, create custom routines, temporarily replace products, track completed routines, review previous days, monitor expiration dates, and keep a personal usage history.

Barcode scanner

The barcode scanner can help fill in product information using community product databases. Not every product is available in those databases.

If a product cannot be found, you can still enter its information manually.

iCloud Sync

Beauty Shelf can synchronize your products, routines, and history through your private iCloud account so your data remains available across supported Apple devices.

Purchases

Beauty Shelf includes a 30-day free trial with access to all features.

After the trial ends, the app can be permanently unlocked with a single one-time purchase. There is no subscription.

If you already purchased the permanent unlock, use Restore Purchases inside the app.
