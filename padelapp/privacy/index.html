Padel App Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Padel App handles information related to its users across its mobile and smartwatch applications.

1. Contact

Application: Padel App
Email: supportappic@gmail.com

For questions related to privacy, data handling, or the operation of Padel App, you may contact us using the email address provided above.

2. General information

Padel App allows users to organize and keep track of padel matches and tournaments, including Classic and Americano game modes.

The application is available on supported mobile devices and may include a companion smartwatch application.

Free features can be used without creating a Padel App account.

Padel App may also offer an optional Premium trial and a one-time Premium purchase that unlocks additional features.

3. Data entered into the application

Users may enter information such as:

  • Player names.
  • Team or pair names.
  • Match results.
  • Tournament settings.
  • Application settings and preferences.

This information is stored locally on the user's device.

Padel App does not send this information to its own servers and does not use it for advertising, profiling, or commercial tracking.

Users are advised not to enter sensitive or unnecessary personal information in fields intended for player or team names.

4. Premium trial and account authorization

Padel App may use platform services to manage eligibility and status for an optional Premium trial.

On platforms where Google authorization is used, Padel App may request access to:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

This permission provides access only to Padel App's private application data area within the user's Google Drive account.

It does not allow Padel App to access the user's normal Google Drive files, such as documents, photographs, videos, or other personal files.

Padel App may store information such as the Premium trial start date in this private application data area for the purpose of managing trial eligibility and duration.

Platform authorization is not used for advertising, profiling, or commercial tracking.

5. Account information

Padel App does not store user passwords or account credentials.

Where authorization services provided by Google, Apple, or another platform provider are used, authentication and credentials are handled by the respective platform provider.

Padel App does not store on its own servers:

  • Account passwords.
  • Payment credentials.
  • Contacts.
  • Personal cloud storage files.
  • Authentication credentials.

6. Local storage

Data related to matches, players, tournaments, settings, history, and preferences is primarily stored locally on the user's device.

The operating system may include locally stored application data in device or cloud backup systems depending on the user's device, operating system, account settings, and backup configuration.

Such backups are managed by the relevant platform provider and are not controlled directly by Padel App.

7. Smartwatch application and synchronization

Padel App may communicate with a companion smartwatch application, including supported Wear OS and watchOS devices.

This communication may synchronize information required for:

  • Match scores.
  • Timers.
  • Match status.
  • Game configuration.
  • Premium feature availability.
  • Other game-related functions.

This communication uses synchronization and connectivity services provided by the relevant operating system and platform.

Padel App does not operate its own server infrastructure for storing match information synchronized between the mobile device and smartwatch.

8. In-app purchases

Padel App may offer an in-app purchase to permanently unlock Premium features.

Unless otherwise indicated in the relevant store, this purchase is a one-time purchase and does not automatically renew.

Purchases are processed by the platform from which the application was obtained, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store.

Padel App does not directly receive or store:

  • Credit or debit card numbers.
  • Bank account information.
  • Payment credentials.
  • Complete payment details.

The platform provider supplies the technical information required to verify, manage, restore, refund, or revoke purchases.

Google's Privacy Policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Apple's Privacy Policy is available at: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

9. RevenueCat

Padel App may use RevenueCat to manage, validate, and restore the status of Premium purchases across supported platforms.

RevenueCat may process technical information associated with purchases, including:

  • Technical application identifiers.
  • Purchased product identifiers.
  • Purchase status.
  • Purchase restoration information.
  • Refund information.
  • Revocation information.
  • Technical information required to determine Premium entitlement.

RevenueCat does not receive through Padel App the player names, team names, match results, or tournament information entered by users for organizing games.

Payments continue to be processed by the relevant application store or platform provider.

10. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

Padel App:

  • Does not contain third-party advertising.
  • Does not sell personal data.
  • Does not create advertising profiles of users.
  • Does not track users across third-party applications or websites for advertising purposes.
  • Does not use user-entered match information for advertising purposes.

11. Support communications

If a user voluntarily contacts Padel App support by email, the following information may be processed:

  • The email address used to contact support.
  • The content of the message.
  • Any information or files voluntarily provided by the user.

This information is used only to respond to inquiries, provide support, investigate problems, or resolve technical issues related to Padel App.

Support communications are not used for advertising purposes and are not sold to third parties.

12. Permissions and third-party services

Padel App uses only permissions and platform services required to provide its functionality.

Depending on the user's device, platform, and features used, services may include:

  • Google Play.
  • Google Play Services.
  • Google Authorization.
  • Google Drive API.
  • Apple App Store services.
  • Apple system services.
  • Android.
  • iOS.
  • Wear OS.
  • watchOS.
  • RevenueCat.

When Google Drive is used, access is limited to the drive.appdata permission and Padel App's private application data area.

13. Children's privacy

Padel App is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising, profiling, or commercial tracking purposes.

Player names entered for the purpose of organizing matches are stored locally on the user's device.

Users responsible for organizing matches should avoid entering unnecessary personal information about minors.

14. Security

Padel App limits the processing of information to the information necessary to provide its functionality.

Locally stored information is protected by the security mechanisms provided by the user's device and operating system.

Communications with external services such as Google, Apple, and RevenueCat use the secure communication mechanisms provided by those services.

No electronic storage or communication system can guarantee absolute security.

15. Third-party services

Padel App relies on services provided by third parties, which may include Google, Apple, and RevenueCat.

These providers may process technical information necessary to provide their services according to their own privacy policies, terms, and data processing practices.

Padel App does not control the independent privacy practices of these third-party service providers.

16. Data deletion and privacy inquiries

Because match, player, tournament, history, and configuration information is primarily stored locally on the user's device, Padel App does not normally have remote access to this information.

Local application information can generally be removed by deleting the relevant information within the application where such functionality is available, clearing application data where supported by the operating system, or uninstalling the application.

Information stored by platform providers may also be subject to the user's Google, Apple, device backup, or other platform settings.

For questions regarding privacy or information voluntarily provided through support communications, users may contact:

supportappic@gmail.com
